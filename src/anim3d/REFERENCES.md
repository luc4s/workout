# Animation references

segment lengths from D. A. Winter, *Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement*.

- Mistake demos: Half reps — not returning to a full hang

## assisted-pullup

- [free-exercise-db — Band Assisted Pull-Up (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Band_Assisted_Pull-Up/0.jpg) — Grip width, band under the foot, bent knees, top and bottom positions.

## dip

- [free-exercise-db — Dips (chest version) (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Dips_-_Chest_Version/0.jpg) — Lockout and bottom positions, forward lean, bent legs.
- [free-exercise-db — Parallel Bar Dip (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Parallel_Bar_Dip/0.jpg) — Bar height and hand placement.
- Mistake demos: Sinking too deep — shoulders roll forward

## hanging-leg-raise

- [free-exercise-db — Hanging Leg Raise (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Hanging_Leg_Raise/0.jpg) — Hang and top positions: thighs past horizontal, knees bent, pelvis tucked.
- Mistake demos: Swinging — momentum does the work

## pushup

- [free-exercise-db — Pushups (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Pushups/0.jpg) — Top and bottom positions, hand placement, straight body line.
- [ACE — Perfecting the push-up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/7265/perfecting-the-push-up-for-all-levels/) — Elbows ~45° from the torso, rigid plank.
- Mistake demos: Hips sagging — lower back arches

## deficit-pushup

- [free-exercise-db — Pushups (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Pushups/0.jpg) — Plank and bottom positions; the deficit lowers the chest below the hands.
- [Deficit Push-Ups – Fitness Volt](https://fitnessvolt.com/deficit-push-ups/) — Hands on raised supports, chest lowers past hand level, body rigid.

## archer-pushup

- [free-exercise-db — Single-Arm Push-Up (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Single-Arm_Push-Up/0.jpg) — Body stays square while loading one arm.

## pike-pushup

- [free-exercise-db — Handstand Push-Ups (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Handstand_Push-Ups/0.jpg) — Head lowers to the floor in front of the hands, elbows track back.
- [Liftosaur — Bodyweight Pike Push-Up](https://www.liftosaur.com/exercises/bodyweight-pike-pushup) — Inverted V, head lowers toward the floor, press back up.
- Mistake demos: Hips drop — becomes a regular push-up

## bulgarian-split-squat

- [free-exercise-db — Suspended Split Squat (rear foot elevated, public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Suspended_Split_Squat/0.jpg) — Rear-foot-elevated top and bottom positions, front shin angle, torso lean.
- [free-exercise-db — Split Squats (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Split_Squats/0.jpg) — Bottom depth: back knee near the floor, front thigh ~parallel.
- Mistake demos: Stance too short — heel lifts, knee shoots forward

## pistol-squat

- [free-exercise-db — Kettlebell Pistol Squat (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Kettlebell_Pistol_Squat/0.jpg) — Single-leg stance, free leg forward, bottom depth and forward reach.
- Mistake demos: Heel lifts — weight rolls onto the toes

## archer-squat

- [free-exercise-db — Barbell Side Split Squat (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Barbell_Side_Split_Squat/0.jpg) — Stance width, toes out, bottom position over one leg.

## rowing-stroke

- [free-exercise-db — Rowing, Stationary (public domain)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/Rowing_Stationary/0.jpg) — Catch and finish positions on the erg.
